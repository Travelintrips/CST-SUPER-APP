---
name: Angkasa Pura vendor tax policy
description: Calculation policy for PT Angkasa Pura Indonesia vendor invoices.
---

For PT Angkasa Pura Indonesia invoices, calculate PPN as 11% of each component DPP. Apply PPh 15% to concession DPP and PPh 4(2) 10% to electricity and water DPP. Supplier cash payment is rounded gross invoice minus rounded withholding; withholding remains a tax liability.

**Why:** The invoice is gross including PPN, while PPh is withheld from the DPP-based tax object. Reusing PPN as PPh or subtracting PPh from DPP produces an incorrect supplier payment.

**How to apply:** Keep component-level DPP, PPN, gross, withholding type/rate/amount, and payable values visible. Preserve manual tax review and proof-of-withholding requirements even when the amounts are calculated automatically.
